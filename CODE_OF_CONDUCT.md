# Code of Conduct

The CCHIS project is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone who contributes code, documentation, data-quality feedback, research, or operational insight.

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate with respect and assume good faith;
- welcome different backgrounds, roles, and levels of experience;
- explain disagreements with evidence and keep discussion focused on the work;
- protect confidential health, contact, and operational information; and
- follow the project security and data-handling guidance.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data, impersonation, and disruptive or abusive conduct in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when this code is violated.
